How it works

How a FiabaMia story is born

FiabaMia writes personalized bedtime stories for children aged 0 to 10: original stories in Italian, ready in about 30 seconds, to read aloud together.

01

A story made to measure

Choose the hero's name (even more than one, for brothers and sisters), their age, the value you want to pass on, the setting and the length: short, medium or long, from about 3 to 7 minutes of reading.

02

Music and interactive sounds

Every story has background music chosen to match its mood. Some words turn golden: tap them to hear the sound, and the music softens to make room for it.

03

Voice guidance

Each chapter has a color that suggests how to read it: green calm, yellow cheerful, blue steady, red quick. Even someone who has never read aloud becomes a storyteller.

04

Let's talk about it

After reading, you'll find three questions to ask your child out loud, tied to the value and the characters: a prompt to talk it over before turning off the light.

Frequently asked questions

Everything you want to know

What is FiabaMia?+

A web app that creates personalized bedtime stories for children aged 0 to 10: choose the name, age, value, setting and mood, and in about 30 seconds you get an original story to read together, with music, interactive sounds and voice guidance.

How much does FiabaMia cost?+

When you sign up you get 3 free Stars, and each story uses 1 Star. You can earn more by inviting other parents or, when available, with the Star packs.

What ages are the stories for?+

From 0 to 10, in five age groups (0–2, 3–4, 5–6, 7–8, 9–10). It's not just the hero that changes: language, sentence length, conflict and the depth of the moral all adapt.

Are the stories safe for children?+

Yes. They're made for bedtime: no violence, nothing scary or inappropriate. Every story passes on an educational value you choose, such as courage, kindness or patience.

What are interactive sounds and voice colors?+

Some words are circled as if in pencil: when your child taps them, they hear the real sound. Voice colors suggest to the parent how to shape tone and rhythm chapter by chapter.

Do I need an account to create a story?+

Yes, a free one: just your email, no password. You receive a sign-in link or code, and your stories stay saved in your personal library.
