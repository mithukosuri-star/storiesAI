HOW IT WORKS

How a storiesAI story is born

A story made to measure

Choose the hero's name, age, a value, a setting, a mood and the length. Add a little villain who makes amends, if you like.

Music and interactive sounds

Background music matched to the mood. Golden words you can tap: wind, a distant storm, small bells — the music softens on its own.

Voice guidance

Each chapter suggests a way to read it: calm, cheerful, clear or quick. Even someone who has never read aloud becomes a storyteller.

Five age groups

From lulling sound words for the smallest to real metaphors and small ethical dilemmas for the older ones.

Let's talk about it

Three questions below the story to ask out loud before turning off the light.

Safe, and yours alone

No violence, nothing scary. Made on your device, kept in your library, works with the internet off.
